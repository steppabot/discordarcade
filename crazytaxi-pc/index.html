An Xbox or PlayStation controller works too: left stick steers, right trigger is gas, left trigger brakes. On a phone or tablet a pad appears on screen. The game (about 120 MB) downloads once and then loads from this browser.

Beta: the Dreamcast Crazy Taxi recompiled to WebAssembly with the Dreamcast Port Kit (built on dcrecomp), not a full emulator. Works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari 17+. Sign in on the arcade first.
