An Xbox or PlayStation controller works too. On a phone or tablet, an N64 pad appears on screen: the arrows on the left walk and strafe, and the right side of the screen is the stick (look and aim). Settings live in the game's PC Options menu on the file-select screen; they and your save files stay in this browser.

Beta: the GoldenEye 007 PC port compiled to WebAssembly, not an emulator. Works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on computers, phones and tablets. Sign in on the arcade first.
